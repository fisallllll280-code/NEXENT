# NEXENT Financial Research Map

NEXENT financial work is organized as research domains, not as an undifferentiated money subsystem.

1. MARKET_RESEARCH
   - prices, liquidity, market structure, data provenance
2. ECONOMIC_MODELING
   - scenarios, macro assumptions, sensitivity
3. RISK_ENGINEERING
   - exposure, stress, failure modes, residuals
4. TREASURY_SIMULATION
   - balances, transfers, settlement states, invariants
5. ACCOUNTING_PRECISION
   - Decimal/minor-unit adapters and reconciliation
6. PORTFOLIO/ALLOCATION_RESEARCH
   - constrained allocation and optimization experiments
7. FINANCIAL_ARCHITECTURE
   - contracts, ledgers, controls, recovery, auditability
8. PROOF_AND_ASSURANCE
   - invariants, deterministic replay, evidence, falsification

Cross-domain rule: research artifacts may compose, but each composition must preserve provenance and assumptions.
