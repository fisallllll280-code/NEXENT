# ARC-X Ω Boundary — NEXENT Integration v1

**Status:** SPECIFIED

NEXENT is the discovery/search/design/evolution candidate plane. ARC-X is the governed reconstruction and epistemic compilation boundary in VAIXLNS.

## NEXENT may provide
- architecture search candidates;
- capability-gap discoveries;
- architecture genome variants;
- recombination candidates;
- counterfactual and adversarial experiment plans;
- research evidence and synthesis outputs;
- evolution proposals.

## NEXENT may not provide
- canonical authority;
- unilateral promotion to production;
- silent mutation of VAIXLNS canonical state;
- proof by assertion.

## Boundary

```text
NEXENT
  ↓
Candidate
  ↓
ARC-X
  ↓
EIR + provenance + proof obligations
  ↓
VV verification
  ↓
VAIXLNS admission
  ↓
VX execution
```

All candidate outputs remain candidate/proposed until the governing evidence and admission path establishes a later state.
