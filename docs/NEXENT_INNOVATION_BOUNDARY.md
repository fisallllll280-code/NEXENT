# NEXENT Innovation Boundary

NEXENT is a native architecture discovery, research, synthesis and engineering system.

## NEXENT owns

- architecture search and candidate generation
- system discovery and missing-system detection
- engineering decomposition and synthesis
- specialist-agent coordination
- contradiction and impact analysis
- simulation-oriented research
- evidence and replay research primitives
- innovation inventory and research provenance

## NEXENT does not silently own

- VAIXLNS constitutional authority
- final canonical adoption into VAIXLNS
- production policy authority for VAIXLNS
- mutation of another repository without an explicit integration contract

## State boundary

SOURCE-ASSERTED -> DESIGN -> IMPLEMENTED -> TESTED -> VERIFIED -> APPROVED

Crossing from NEXENT research to VAIXLNS canonical status requires an explicit adoption record supported by evidence.

## Separation principle

One innovation identity, many implementations/research artifacts, one canonical owner after adoption.
